# Dafinitiq application answers

## Product name
CaseLens AI

## Describe your AI product
CaseLens AI is an open-source legal document intelligence workspace for lawyers, researchers and legal-aid teams. Users can upload case files or judgments and receive document summaries, timelines, key facts, grounded question answering and source citations. The MVP uses Python, Streamlit, PyMuPDF, scikit-learn retrieval and the open-source FLAN-T5 model, with no paid API required. A production version could later add stronger embedding models, a vector database and larger open-source LLMs.

## Why did you choose this idea?
I chose this idea because I have already worked on major components of a real legal AI platform involving Dubai Court data, including scraping, preprocessing, retrieval, RAG, Llama 2 13B-based legal Q&A, backend integration and an ML-based case outcome prediction component. That experience showed me how much time legal professionals spend searching and cross-referencing information, and I want to build a more accessible open-source tool that keeps answers grounded in source documents.
